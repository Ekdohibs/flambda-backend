# S7, the reaper: status

The reaper (`middle_end/flambda2/reaper/`) is the whole-unit pass that
removes what is not used and unboxes what can be: a traversal builds a
flow graph of the unit (`traverse.ml`, `traverse_acc.ml`), Datalog rules
are solved over it (`points_to_analysis.ml`, `unboxing_analysis.ml`), and
the unit is rebuilt from the solution (`rebuild.ml`), with the types of
what the unit exports rewritten (`types_rewriter.ml`).

`PARALLEL-PLAN.md` kept S7 for later, with the idea of checking the
reaper's output with `validate_unit`, the analysis left unverified. This
stream does something else, on the human's request: the analysis is
modelled and its solution given a meaning, so that the rebuild can be
proved correct for any solution that is closed under the rules. The
solution is an input, as a decision of Simplify is, and nothing is
believed of it but that it is closed: the least solution, which the
Datalog engine computes, is.

A model of the same reaper on a language of its own was written before
this stream, outside this tree, with the proofs of the same statements
for that language and the counterexamples that it found. This stream
carries it over to the semantics of this tree, one part at a time.

## The plan

Each step is a pair of files, or three, after `rocq/HACKING.md`.

1. The flow graph and the rules (`FlambdaReaperGraphSpec.v`): the
   nodes, the fields, the relations of the graph, the tables of the
   solution, and what it is for the tables to be closed under the rules
   of `points_to_analysis.ml`. No term in it.
2. The traversal (`FlambdaReaperTraversalSpec.v`): what the graph must
   hold for a unit, construct by construct, after `traverse.ml`. A
   relation between a term and a graph, so that the statements hold of
   every graph that has at least these facts.
3. The meaning of a solution (`FlambdaReaperFlowSpec.v` and its
   proofs): a relation between the values of the machine
   (`FlambdaSemanticsSpec.step`) and the nodes, kept by every step of a
   unit whose graph is closed. The machine has no identity of
   allocations, so the relation says which allocation a value may come
   from rather than reading it off the value.
4. The rebuild without changes of calling convention
   (`FlambdaReaperRebuild.v`, its Spec and its proofs): unused `Let`s
   removed, unused fields of blocks and unused boxed numbers made
   poison, unused value slots removed, unused parameters of
   continuations removed. The statement is that the rebuilt unit
   refines the unit, run on its own (`program_refines`), up to what an
   exit can observe.
5. Then, one at a time: changes of calling convention, unboxing of
   blocks and of closures, tupled code (`traverse_acc.ml`,
   `create_unknown_arity_tupled_call_witnesses`, with the edge that
   keeps a tuple alive, and an example that shows the edge is needed),
   exceptions, partial and over-applications, and the rewriting of the
   types.
